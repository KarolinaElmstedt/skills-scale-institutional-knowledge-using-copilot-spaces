# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads coordinate the day-to-day execution of work so the team can deliver predictable increments. They work across planning, staffing, and dependency management to reduce blockers and keep momentum high.

### Responsibilities
- Own sprint and milestone planning coordination
- Track delivery progress, risks, and dependencies
- Remove blockers and escalate issues when necessary
- Partner with PMs and engineering leads on capacity and delivery trade-offs
- Help maintain realistic commitments for scope and dates

### Goals
- Keep work moving smoothly through the delivery pipeline
- Improve predictability and transparency across the team
- Reduce delivery friction caused by unclear ownership or unresolved blockers

### Typical Communication
- Standups, sprint planning, and weekly cross-functional syncs
- Delivery status reporting and dependency tracking
- Coordination with PMs, Product Managers, and engineering leaders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers on schedule and stakeholder communication
- Collaborates with Product Managers on priority and trade-offs
- Supports Developers by identifying bottlenecks and sequencing work
- Partners with QA to align release readiness and test coverage

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers lead the technical health and performance of the engineering organization. They balance delivery needs with engineering quality, team capability, and sustainable execution.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical planning, architecture, and engineering standards
- Support team capacity planning and staffing decisions
- Coach developers and strengthen team execution practices
- Ensure quality, operational stability, and code health
- Partner on risk mitigation for technical dependencies and delivery constraints

### Goals
- Maintain a healthy, high-performing engineering team
- Improve delivery quality and reduce rework
- Support sustainable technical execution over time

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and team reviews
- Architecture and design discussions
- Capacity, staffing, and escalations with project leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on mentoring, technical execution, and quality expectations
- Coordinates with Delivery Leads on sequencing, capacity, and trade-offs
- Partners with Project Managers on risks, resourcing, and cross-team dependencies
- Collaborates with Security/Compliance Owners to ensure technical controls and mitigation plans are practical

---

## UX / Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Design Leads shape the user experience and ensure the product remains usable, accessible, and aligned with customer needs. They help translate user and business needs into usable, testable product decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Lead user research and experience strategy
- Define interaction patterns, design direction, and accessibility considerations
- Translate customer pain points into product experience requirements
- Support design handoff and validation across delivery stages
- Partner with stakeholders on prioritization of experience improvements

### Goals
- Improve customer value and usability
- Ensure design quality throughout the product lifecycle
- Reduce rework by aligning design decisions earlier in delivery

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews, discovery workshops, and stakeholder alignment
- Feedback loops with Product Managers and engineering teams
- Quality validation with QA and acceptance criteria reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to refine customer needs and success outcomes
- Works with Developers on implementation details, design feasibility, and usability QA
- Supports Project Managers with milestone planning and release-readiness dependencies
- Aligns with QA on validation of user experience and accessibility requirements

---

## Customer Success Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer Success Liaisons represent the voice of the customer, especially around adoption, feedback, and service quality. They help teams understand what customers need and how to improve the experience post-launch.

### Responsibilities
- Gather customer feedback and surface issues that affect adoption or satisfaction
- Coordinate beta programs, customer validation, and launch readiness feedback
- Share usage patterns, pain points, and support trends with product teams
- Support release communication and customer-facing readiness
- Identify opportunities for customer education and product improvement

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Ensure customer feedback informs roadmap and delivery decisions
- Support smoother handoff between product development and customer adoption

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback sessions, stakeholder reviews, and launch updates
- Weekly or milestone-based customer insights summaries
- Cross-functional coordination with Product Managers and support teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate customer needs into roadmap decisions
- Supports Project Managers during release communication and stakeholder updates
- Provides feedback to Developers and QA about usability, real-world workflows, and support pain points
- Helps identify customer-impacting risks and escalate issues early

---

## Security / Compliance Owners

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Owners ensure that projects meet required controls, risk standards, and secure operating practices. They help teams identify and reduce security and compliance risk without slowing delivery unnecessarily.

### Responsibilities
- Define or validate security and compliance requirements
- Coordinate threat modeling, security reviews, and audit preparation
- Monitor project risk exposure and remediation plans
- Partner with engineering on secure implementation and validation
- Support incident readiness and response coordination

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Improve trust and operational resilience
- Make policy requirements clear and actionable for delivery teams

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews, compliance checkpoints, and risk assessments
- Escalations for vulnerabilities or policy exceptions
- Coordination with project leaders and engineering stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on secure architecture and remediation
- Partners with Engineering Managers on technical controls and delivery constraints
- Supports Project Managers with risk tracking and stakeholder escalation
- Collaborates with Product Managers on trade-offs between speed, security, and business impact

---

## Data Analysts / Analytics Owners

### Role Summary
Data Analysts or Analytics Owners help the team define and measure success. They ensure the right metrics exist, are trusted, and can inform decisions during delivery and after launch.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, instrumentation requirements, and reporting needs
- Validate that tracking is accurate and useful for decision-making
- Analyze launch performance and product outcomes
- Identify trends, risks, and opportunities based on usage and quality data
- Support product and roadmap decisions with evidence

### Goals
- Improve decision quality with reliable measurement
- Help teams learn from real usage and customer behavior
- Reduce guesswork in prioritization and validation

### Typical Communication
- KPI reviews, reporting dashboards, and milestone analysis
- Coordination with Product Managers and engineering teams
- Input into launch, release, and retrospective reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to define and validate success metrics
- Collaborates with Developers on instrumentation and reporting quality
- Supports Project Managers with milestone and release tracking
- Helps QA and stakeholders understand whether changes achieved the intended outcome

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles help clarify accountability, handoffs, and escalation paths across product, engineering, quality, and customer-facing work.
